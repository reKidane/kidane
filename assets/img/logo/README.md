# RESILORIS identity

## Two-level system
- **Emblem** (primary, about 160 px and larger): the full story. A continuous ring in which heat, drought and salinity blend into each other at the joints (multifactorial, co-occurring or sequential stress), each with its symbol (sun, crossed-out drop, Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions); DNA-helix stem; chromatogram as the ground line; the cell at the stem base as the hub of a network linking shoot, cereal and roots; a dicot with a four-petal (Arabidopsis-type) flower beside a cereal with grain and awns.
- The emblem is also the O whenever RESILORIS is written (horizontal logo, wordmark, stacked logo).
- **Simple mark** (small uses): the leaf-O, for favicons, avatars and the O inside the horizontal wordmark.

## Files
- `resiloris-stacked-*.svg`: primary logo, emblem above the name and tagline. Use on posters, title slides, the website hero, reports and letterheads with room.
- `resiloris-emblem-*.svg`: emblem alone, for badges, stickers, cover pages and social banners.
- `resiloris-logo-*.svg`: horizontal logo, name with the emblem as the O, plus tagline. Use where it can be at least 80 px tall.
- `resiloris-wordmark-*.svg`: name with the emblem as the O, no tagline.
- `resiloris-wordmark-small-*.svg`: name with the simple mark as the O, for slide footers and anything under about 60 px tall.
- `resiloris-mark-*.svg`: the leaf-O mark on its own, for 48 px and larger.
- `resiloris-favicon*.svg`, `resiloris-favicon-32/64.png`: simplified mark for 32 px and smaller (browser tabs).
- `resiloris-app-icon*.svg/png`: mark on a rounded tile, for avatars, social profiles and touch icons (180 px).
- Versions: `light` (default, on white), `dark` (on dark backgrounds), `mono` (one colour, print and fax), `monoWhite` (reversed on photos or dark colour).
- PNGs have transparent backgrounds except the app icon.

## Colours
- Ink (wordmark): #1F4D3A
- Leaf: #2E6A4C
- Root network: #3D3A6B
- Simple-mark ring: the same continuous heat, drought and salinity blend as the emblem
- Tagline: #4D5D55
- Ring stress colours (blended continuously in OKLab between them): heat #E4572E (sun #F2A541), drought #2F80C2, salinity #7E57C2
- Ions: Na⁺ #7E57C2 (violet), Cl⁻ #2F9E57 (green), following the standard CPK element colours
- Cereal grain: #C9A13B

## Type
- Wordmark: Cinzel Medium, letter-spaced, converted to outlines (no font needed).
- Tagline and band labels: IBM Plex Sans (Regular and SemiBold), converted to outlines. Use IBM Plex Sans for any text set next to the logo.

## Rules
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the mark's ring.
- Do not recolour or break the ring, stretch the logo, or set the mark on busy images without the monoWhite version.
- The full illustration (molecule to field) stays a hero image or cover visual, not the logo.
