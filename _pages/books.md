Core references from established publishers. Books marked **open access** are free to read in full; the rest are available through institutional subscriptions on SpringerLink and ScienceDirect.

## Plant physiology and photosynthesis

- **[Plant Physiology and Development, 6th edition](https://learninglink.oup.com/access/taiz-plant-physiology-6e)**. Taiz L., Zeiger E., Møller I.M. and Murphy A. Sinauer, Oxford University Press, 2015. The standard plant physiology textbook: water and solute transport, photosynthesis, stomatal biology, nutrient assimilation, hormones and development from germination to senescence.
- **[Plant Physiological Ecology, 3rd edition](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-29639-1)**. Lambers H. and Oliveira R.S. Springer, 2019. Carbon metabolism, water relations, energy balance and mineral nutrition, scaled from leaf to canopy and ecosystem.
- **[Photosynthesis: Physiology and Metabolism](https://doi.org/10.1007/0-306-48137-5)**. Leegood R.C., Sharkey T.D. and von Caemmerer S. (eds). Springer, 2000, Advances in Photosynthesis and Respiration vol. 9. The Calvin cycle, Rubisco, photorespiration, sucrose and starch metabolism, and C4 photosynthesis.

## Plant metabolism and metabolomics

- **[Plant Biochemistry, 4th edition](https://shop.elsevier.com/books/catalog/isbn/9780123849878)**. Heldt H.W. and Piechulla B. Elsevier, Academic Press, 2010. Primary and specialised metabolism, from photosynthesis to secondary compounds.
- **[Plant Metabolomics](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-29782-0)**. Saito K., Dixon R.A. and Willmitzer L. (eds). Springer, 2006, Biotechnology in Agriculture and Forestry vol. 57. The founding volume of plant metabolomics, by the leaders who shaped the field.
- **[Plant Metabolomics: Methods and Protocols](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-7819-9)**. António C. (ed). Springer, Humana, 2018, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 1778. Laboratory protocols for GC-MS, LC-MS and NMR plant metabolomics.
- **[Processing Metabolomics and Proteomics Data with Open Software](https://doi.org/10.1039/9781788019880)**. Royal Society of Chemistry, 2020. GC-MS, LC-MS and lipidomics data processing, statistics and metabolite annotation with open tools.

## Ionomics and mineral nutrition

- **[Marschner's Mineral Nutrition of Plants, 4th edition](https://doi.org/10.1016/C2019-0-00491-8)**. Rengel Z., Cakmak I. and White P.J. (eds). Elsevier, Academic Press, 2023. Ion uptake, long-distance transport, nutrient functions and stress physiology; the reference for ionomics and salinity.
- **[Plant Ionomics: Sensing, Signaling and Regulation](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en/areas-interest/natural-sciences/plant-ionomics-978-1-119-80301-0)**. Singh V.P. and Siddiqui M.H. (eds). Wiley, 2023. Nutrient uptake, transport and signalling, nutrient regulation of metabolites and gene expression, and ionomics of abiotic stress tolerance.

## Transcriptomics and single-cell omics

- **[Transcriptome Data Analysis](https://link.springer.com/978-1-0716-3885-9)**. Springer, Humana, 2024, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 2812. RNA-seq pipelines, gene interaction networks, multi-omics integration and single-cell trajectories.
- **[Single Cell Transcriptomics: Methods and Protocols](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-0716-2756-3)**. Calogero R.A. and Benes V. (eds). Springer, Humana, 2023, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 2584. Single-cell RNA-seq from library preparation to data analysis.

## Spatial omics

- **[Imaging Mass Spectrometry: Methods and Protocols, 2nd edition](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-0716-3319-9)**. Cole L.M. and Clench M.R. (eds). Springer, Humana, 2023, Methods in Molecular Biology. Spatial metabolomics and elemental imaging by MALDI-2, nano-DESI and LA-ICP-MS.
- **[In Situ Hybridization Protocols, 6th edition](https://link.springer.com/book/9781071652718)**. Springer, Humana, 2026, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 3036. RNA detection in tissue, 3D imaging, spatial transcriptomics approaches and image quantification.
- **[Orchestrating Spatial Transcriptomics Analysis with Bioconductor](https://bioconductor.org/books/release/OSTA/)**. Bioconductor, **free online**. Workflows for sequencing-based and imaging-based spatial omics data.

## Genomics and bioinformatics

- **[Plant Genomics: Methods and Protocols](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-7003-2)**. Busch W. (ed). Springer, Humana, 2017, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 1610. Linking phenotypes to their molecular and genomic bases in plants.
- **[Plant Bioinformatics: Methods and Protocols](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-3167-5)**. Edwards D. (ed). Springer, Humana, 2016, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 1374. Databases, tools and pipelines for high-throughput plant data.

## Plant breeding and genomic prediction

- **[Principles of Plant Genetics and Breeding, 3rd edition](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en/areas-interest/natural-sciences/principles-of-plant-genetics-and-breeding-978-1-119-62632-9)**. Acquaah G. Wiley, 2020. Classical and molecular breeding, quantitative genetics, genome editing, and breeding chapters for wheat, maize and sorghum.
- **[Genomic Prediction of Complex Traits: Methods and Protocols](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-0716-2205-6)**. Ahmadi N. and Bartholomé J. (eds). Springer, Humana, 2022, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 2467. From QTL mapping to genomic, phenomic and multi-omics prediction in plant breeding.
- **[Multivariate Statistical Machine Learning Methods for Genomic Prediction](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-89010-0)**. Montesinos López O.A., Montesinos López A. and Crossa J. Springer, 2022, **open access**. Genomic selection models, from Bayesian regression to deep learning, with worked R examples.

## Stress tolerance

- **[Drought Stress Tolerance in Plants, Vol. 1: Physiology and Biochemistry](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-28899-4)**. Hossain M.A., Wani S.H., Bhattacharjee S., Burritt D.J. and Tran L.S.P. (eds). Springer, 2016. Hormones, ROS signalling, osmolytes, photosynthesis and physiological breeding under drought.
- **[Salinity Responses and Tolerance in Plants, Vol. 1](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-75671-4)**. Kumar V., Wani S.H., Suprasanna P. and Tran L.S.P. (eds). Springer, 2018. Sensory, ion-transport and signalling mechanisms as targets for crop salt tolerance.
- **[Heat Stress Tolerance in Plants](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en/areas-interest/natural-sciences/heat-stress-tolerance-in-plants-978-1-119-43236-4)**. Wani S.H. and Kumar V. (eds). Wiley, 2020. Physiological, molecular and genetic bases of thermotolerance, with transcriptomics and germplasm screening.

## Omics data analysis

- **[Modern Statistics for Modern Biology](https://www.huber.embl.de/msmb/)**. Holmes S. and Huber W. Cambridge University Press, 2019, **free online**. Multivariate analysis, clustering, testing and networks for high-throughput biology in R.

## Grapevine

- **[The Science of Grapevines, 3rd edition](https://shop.elsevier.com/books/the-science-of-grapevines/keller/978-0-12-816365-8)**. Keller M. Elsevier, Academic Press, 2020. Grapevine anatomy, phenology, water relations, photosynthesis, partitioning and stress physiology.
- **[Grapevine in a Changing Environment](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en/areas-interest/natural-sciences/grapevine-in-a-changing-environment-978-1-118-73605-0)**. Gerós H., Chaves M.M., Medrano H. and Delrot S. (eds). Wiley, 2016. Molecular and ecophysiological responses to drought, heat, salinity and cold, and the role of rootstocks.
- **[The Grape Genome](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-18601-2)**. Cantu D. and Walker M.A. (eds). Springer, 2019, Compendium of Plant Genomes. Genome assembly, transcriptomics, water relations, berry ripening and rootstock breeding.
- **[Grapevine Breeding Programs for the Wine Industry](https://shop.elsevier.com/books/grapevine-breeding-programs-for-the-wine-industry/reynolds/978-1-78242-075-0)**. Reynolds A.G. (ed). Elsevier, Woodhead, 2015. Traditional and molecular breeding techniques and breeding programs across wine regions.

## Sorghum

- **[The Sorghum Genome](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-47789-3)**. Rakshit S. and Wang Y.H. (eds). Springer, 2016, Compendium of Plant Genomes. Sorghum genome sequencing, genomic resources and their use in breeding.
- **[Sorghum in the 21st Century](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-15-8249-3)**. Tonapi V.A., Talwar H.S., Are A.K., Bhat B.V., Reddy C.R. and Dalton T.J. (eds). Springer, 2020. Genetic diversity, breeding for yield, adaptation and stress, phenotyping and hybrid seed production.
- **[Sorghum: Methods and Protocols](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-9039-9)**. Zhao Z.Y. and Dahlberg J. (eds). Springer, Humana, 2019, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 1931. Drought QTL mapping, KASP marker-assisted selection, mutant libraries and genome editing.

## Arabidopsis

- **[Arabidopsis Protocols, 4th edition](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-0716-0880-7)**. Sanchez-Serrano J.J. and Salinas J. (eds). Springer, Humana, 2021, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 2200. Natural variation, GWAS, CRISPR, imaging, and genome, transcriptome and metabolome approaches.

## Wheat and barley

- **[Wheat Improvement: Food Security in a Changing Climate](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-90673-3)**. Reynolds M.P. and Braun H.J. (eds). Springer, 2022, **open access**. Breeding methods, genetic gain, physiology and phenotyping for climate resilience in small-grain cereals.
- **[The Barley Genome](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-92528-8)**. Stein N. and Muehlbauer G.J. (eds). Springer, 2018, Compendium of Plant Genomes. Genome sequencing, germplasm mining and genomic tools for barley improvement.
- **[Barley: Production, Improvement, and Uses](https://gov.wiley.com/WileyCDA/WileyTitle/productCd-0813801230.html)**. Ullrich S.E. (ed). Wiley-Blackwell, 2011. Physiology, genetics, breeding, and management of biotic and abiotic stresses in barley.

## Maize

- **[The Maize Genome](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-97427-9)**. Bennetzen J., Flint-Garcia S., Hirsch C. and Tuberosa R. (eds). Springer, 2018, Compendium of Plant Genomes. Pan-genome, methylome, genotyping and genomics-assisted breeding for drought and cold tolerance.
- **[Handbook of Maize: Genetics and Genomics](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-387-77863-1)**. Bennetzen J.L. and Hake S. (eds). Springer, 2009. Maize as a model for plant genetics, genome structure and crop improvement.

## Citrus

- **[The Genus Citrus](https://doi.org/10.1016/C2016-0-02375-6)**. Talon M., Caruso M. and Gmitter F.G. (eds). Elsevier, Woodhead, 2020. Citrus genome, rootstocks, breeding, and responses to salinity, water deficit and changing climates.
- **[The Citrus Genome](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-15308-3)**. Gentile A., La Malfa S. and Deng Z. (eds). Springer, 2020, Compendium of Plant Genomes. Citrus species, rootstocks and the application of genomics to citrus breeding.
