## Collaboration Opportunities

- Joint research on grapevine responses to salinity, water deficit, heatwave stress, and combined abiotic stress.
- Multi-omics integration involving metabolomics, ionomics, transcriptomics, phenomics, and physiological datasets.
- UAV thermal imaging and field phenotyping for crop stress detection.
- Biological network analysis and machine-learning models for crop performance prediction.
- Manuscript preparation, grant proposals, conference presentations, and student exchange.
