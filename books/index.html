To Read

Core books from Springer, Elsevier and other leading publishers on plant stress physiology, metabolomics, omics and crop improvement.

Core references from established publishers. Books marked open access are free to read in full; the rest are available through institutional subscriptions on SpringerLink and ScienceDirect.

Plant physiology and photosynthesis

  • Plant Physiology and Development, 6th edition. Taiz L., Zeiger E., Møller I.M. and Murphy A. Sinauer, Oxford University Press, 2015. The standard plant physiology textbook: water and solute transport, photosynthesis, stomatal biology, nutrient assimilation, hormones and development from germination to senescence.
  • Plant Physiological Ecology, 3rd edition. Lambers H. and Oliveira R.S. Springer, 2019. Carbon metabolism, water relations, energy balance and mineral nutrition, scaled from leaf to canopy and ecosystem.
  • Photosynthesis: Physiology and Metabolism. Leegood R.C., Sharkey T.D. and von Caemmerer S. (eds). Springer, 2000, Advances in Photosynthesis and Respiration vol. 9. The Calvin cycle, Rubisco, photorespiration, sucrose and starch metabolism, and C4 photosynthesis.

Plant metabolism and metabolomics

  • Plant Biochemistry, 4th edition. Heldt H.W. and Piechulla B. Elsevier, Academic Press, 2010. Primary and specialised metabolism, from photosynthesis to secondary compounds.
  • Plant Metabolomics. Saito K., Dixon R.A. and Willmitzer L. (eds). Springer, 2006, Biotechnology in Agriculture and Forestry vol. 57. The founding volume of plant metabolomics, by the leaders who shaped the field.
  • Plant Metabolomics: Methods and Protocols. António C. (ed). Springer, Humana, 2018, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 1778. Laboratory protocols for GC-MS, LC-MS and NMR plant metabolomics.
  • Processing Metabolomics and Proteomics Data with Open Software. Royal Society of Chemistry, 2020. GC-MS, LC-MS and lipidomics data processing, statistics and metabolite annotation with open tools.

Ionomics and mineral nutrition

  • Marschner’s Mineral Nutrition of Plants, 4th edition. Rengel Z., Cakmak I. and White P.J. (eds). Elsevier, Academic Press, 2023. Ion uptake, long-distance transport, nutrient functions and stress physiology; the reference for ionomics and salinity.
  • Plant Ionomics: Sensing, Signaling and Regulation. Singh V.P. and Siddiqui M.H. (eds). Wiley, 2023. Nutrient uptake, transport and signalling, nutrient regulation of metabolites and gene expression, and ionomics of abiotic stress tolerance.

Transcriptomics and single-cell omics

  • Transcriptome Data Analysis. Springer, Humana, 2024, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 2812. RNA-seq pipelines, gene interaction networks, multi-omics integration and single-cell trajectories.
  • Single Cell Transcriptomics: Methods and Protocols. Calogero R.A. and Benes V. (eds). Springer, Humana, 2023, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 2584. Single-cell RNA-seq from library preparation to data analysis.

Spatial omics

  • Imaging Mass Spectrometry: Methods and Protocols, 2nd edition. Cole L.M. and Clench M.R. (eds). Springer, Humana, 2023, Methods in Molecular Biology. Spatial metabolomics and elemental imaging by MALDI-2, nano-DESI and LA-ICP-MS.
  • In Situ Hybridization Protocols, 6th edition. Springer, Humana, 2026, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 3036. RNA detection in tissue, 3D imaging, spatial transcriptomics approaches and image quantification.
  • Orchestrating Spatial Transcriptomics Analysis with Bioconductor. Bioconductor, free online. Workflows for sequencing-based and imaging-based spatial omics data.

Genomics and bioinformatics

  • Plant Genomics: Methods and Protocols. Busch W. (ed). Springer, Humana, 2017, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 1610. Linking phenotypes to their molecular and genomic bases in plants.
  • Plant Bioinformatics: Methods and Protocols. Edwards D. (ed). Springer, Humana, 2016, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 1374. Databases, tools and pipelines for high-throughput plant data.

Plant breeding and genomic prediction

  • Principles of Plant Genetics and Breeding, 3rd edition. Acquaah G. Wiley, 2020. Classical and molecular breeding, quantitative genetics, genome editing, and breeding chapters for wheat, maize and sorghum.
  • Genomic Prediction of Complex Traits: Methods and Protocols. Ahmadi N. and Bartholomé J. (eds). Springer, Humana, 2022, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 2467. From QTL mapping to genomic, phenomic and multi-omics prediction in plant breeding.
  • Multivariate Statistical Machine Learning Methods for Genomic Prediction. Montesinos López O.A., Montesinos López A. and Crossa J. Springer, 2022, open access. Genomic selection models, from Bayesian regression to deep learning, with worked R examples.

Stress tolerance

  • Drought Stress Tolerance in Plants, Vol. 1: Physiology and Biochemistry. Hossain M.A., Wani S.H., Bhattacharjee S., Burritt D.J. and Tran L.S.P. (eds). Springer, 2016. Hormones, ROS signalling, osmolytes, photosynthesis and physiological breeding under drought.
  • Salinity Responses and Tolerance in Plants, Vol. 1. Kumar V., Wani S.H., Suprasanna P. and Tran L.S.P. (eds). Springer, 2018. Sensory, ion-transport and signalling mechanisms as targets for crop salt tolerance.
  • Heat Stress Tolerance in Plants. Wani S.H. and Kumar V. (eds). Wiley, 2020. Physiological, molecular and genetic bases of thermotolerance, with transcriptomics and germplasm screening.

Omics data analysis

  • Modern Statistics for Modern Biology. Holmes S. and Huber W. Cambridge University Press, 2019, free online. Multivariate analysis, clustering, testing and networks for high-throughput biology in R.

Grapevine

  • The Science of Grapevines, 3rd edition. Keller M. Elsevier, Academic Press, 2020. Grapevine anatomy, phenology, water relations, photosynthesis, partitioning and stress physiology.
  • Grapevine in a Changing Environment. Gerós H., Chaves M.M., Medrano H. and Delrot S. (eds). Wiley, 2016. Molecular and ecophysiological responses to drought, heat, salinity and cold, and the role of rootstocks.
  • The Grape Genome. Cantu D. and Walker M.A. (eds). Springer, 2019, Compendium of Plant Genomes. Genome assembly, transcriptomics, water relations, berry ripening and rootstock breeding.
  • Grapevine Breeding Programs for the Wine Industry. Reynolds A.G. (ed). Elsevier, Woodhead, 2015. Traditional and molecular breeding techniques and breeding programs across wine regions.

Sorghum

  • The Sorghum Genome. Rakshit S. and Wang Y.H. (eds). Springer, 2016, Compendium of Plant Genomes. Sorghum genome sequencing, genomic resources and their use in breeding.
  • Sorghum in the 21st Century. Tonapi V.A., Talwar H.S., Are A.K., Bhat B.V., Reddy C.R. and Dalton T.J. (eds). Springer, 2020. Genetic diversity, breeding for yield, adaptation and stress, phenotyping and hybrid seed production.
  • Sorghum: Methods and Protocols. Zhao Z.Y. and Dahlberg J. (eds). Springer, Humana, 2019, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 1931. Drought QTL mapping, KASP marker-assisted selection, mutant libraries and genome editing.

Arabidopsis

  • Arabidopsis Protocols, 4th edition. Sanchez-Serrano J.J. and Salinas J. (eds). Springer, Humana, 2021, Methods in Molecular Biology vol. 2200. Natural variation, GWAS, CRISPR, imaging, and genome, transcriptome and metabolome approaches.

Wheat and barley

  • Wheat Improvement: Food Security in a Changing Climate. Reynolds M.P. and Braun H.J. (eds). Springer, 2022, open access. Breeding methods, genetic gain, physiology and phenotyping for climate resilience in small-grain cereals.
  • The Barley Genome. Stein N. and Muehlbauer G.J. (eds). Springer, 2018, Compendium of Plant Genomes. Genome sequencing, germplasm mining and genomic tools for barley improvement.
  • Barley: Production, Improvement, and Uses. Ullrich S.E. (ed). Wiley-Blackwell, 2011. Physiology, genetics, breeding, and management of biotic and abiotic stresses in barley.

Maize

  • The Maize Genome. Bennetzen J., Flint-Garcia S., Hirsch C. and Tuberosa R. (eds). Springer, 2018, Compendium of Plant Genomes. Pan-genome, methylome, genotyping and genomics-assisted breeding for drought and cold tolerance.
  • Handbook of Maize: Genetics and Genomics. Bennetzen J.L. and Hake S. (eds). Springer, 2009. Maize as a model for plant genetics, genome structure and crop improvement.

Citrus

  • The Genus Citrus. Talon M., Caruso M. and Gmitter F.G. (eds). Elsevier, Woodhead, 2020. Citrus genome, rootstocks, breeding, and responses to salinity, water deficit and changing climates.
  • The Citrus Genome. Gentile A., La Malfa S. and Deng Z. (eds). Springer, 2020, Compendium of Plant Genomes. Citrus species, rootstocks and the application of genomics to citrus breeding.
